### CHANNELS
- **Direct Sales**: Enterprise account executives

- **Event Marketing**: Conferences, webinars
- **Content**: Blog, whitepapers, case studies

### REVENUE STREAMS

**1. SaaS Subscription** (60% of revenue)
- Starter: $500/month
- Professional: $2,000/month
- Enterprise: Custom (starting $10K/month)

**2. API & Usage-Based** (15% of revenue)
- Per-analysis unit: $0.01-$0.50
- Enterprise API: Custom pricing
- Volume discounts for partners

**3. Professional Services** (15% of revenue)
- Consulting: $150-$400/hour
- Custom implementation: $25K-$250K projects
- Training & certification: $5K-$50K

**4. Licensing & Partnerships** (10% of revenue)
- Research licenses: $10K-$100K annually
- White-label: Revenue share 30-50%
- Marketplace commissions: 20-30%

### COST STRUCTURE

**Fixed Costs (40%)**
- Engineering team: $2M/year
- Product & design: $400K/year
- Sales & marketing: $1M/year
- Operations: $300K/year
- Infrastructure baseline: $200K/year

**Variable Costs (60%)**
- Cloud infrastructure: $0.50-$2 per analysis
- Third-party APIs: $100-$500K/month
- Support & customer success: 10% of revenue
- Marketing & acquisition: 25% of revenue

**Unit Economics**
- COGS per analysis: $0.50-$2.00
- Gross Margin: 70-80%
- CAC (Enterprise): $15K-$30K
- LTV (Enterprise): $200K-$1M+
- CAC Payback: 6-12 months
