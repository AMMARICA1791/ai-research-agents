**Status:** draft; not submitted.

## Proposed title
Evidence-First Research — Community

## Short description
An experimental, free AI-agent research workflow for source-linked claims, explicit uncertainty, and human-reviewed conclusions.

## Listing copy
Turn a research question into a reviewable evidence trail. This free community skill helps AI assistants scope questions, collect source provenance, map claims to supporting or contradictory evidence, flag unknowns, and propose reproducible tests.

**Includes:** SKILL.md, workflow documentation, an illustrative example, and a preregistered evaluation rubric.

**Limitations:** This is an instruction protocol, not an automated verification service. Browsing and citation verification depend on the host agent and accessible sources. No measured accuracy improvement has yet been demonstrated. Human review is required.

**Language:** English instructions. Output can be requested in other languages where supported by the host agent.
